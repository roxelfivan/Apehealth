# Apehealth — User guide

> Everything you need to run Apehealth: take a photo of a meal, let AI estimate the calories, and keep a log that never leaves your device.

**Applies to:** `v0.0.3`

---

## What Apehealth is

Apehealth is a **local-first food tracker**. You snap a photo of a meal, an AI model estimates the calories and breaks the meal down into foods, and you save the result to a log that lives in your browser.

There is no account, no sign-up, and no server. Your meals, your goal, and your API key stay in this browser.

## The one-minute version

1. Open Apehealth.
2. Go to **Settings**, choose **Gemini** (the default), paste your API key, press **Save**.
3. Tap **Add meal**, take or upload a photo.
4. Check the numbers, edit anything that looks wrong, press **Save meal**.

That is the whole loop. Everything else — the chart, the editor, the language switch — is optional.

## Conventions used here

- **Settings** is the gear icon in the header, or the gear in the bottom navigation on a phone.
- **Add meal** is the round **+** button in the bottom navigation.
- Menu items are written in **bold** the way they appear on screen.
- Button names differ between English and 繁體中文; this guide uses the English names.
