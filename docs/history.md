# History

> Your calorie log over time — the chart, the day ranges, and the grouped meal list.

## Opening History

Tap **History** in the header (desktop) or the chart icon in the bottom navigation (phone).

## The chart

A bar chart of daily calories for the selected range.

- Each bar is one day.
- The number above a bar is that day's total in kcal.
- The label under a bar is the day of the month.
- A bar in **red** means you went over your daily goal; a bar in the accent colour means you stayed under.
- The chart scales to the larger of your goal and your highest day, so the goal is always a meaningful reference.

If the range has no meals at all, the chart is replaced with **No meals in this range yet.**

## Ranges

Three buttons sit above the chart:

| Range | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **7 days** | The last week, including today. |
| **14 days** | The last fortnight. |
| **30 days** | The last month. |

The range also decides which meals appear in the list below the chart. Days with no meals are still shown in the chart, as empty bars.

## The meal list

Meals in History are grouped by day, newest first. Each group shows:

- The date, formatted for your language (for example `Tue, Nov 17` or `11月18日週三`)
- The day's total in kcal
- The meals themselves, in the same card format as the Today screen

Tap the pencil on any meal to edit it, or the trash to delete it. See [Editing meals](editing-meals.md).

## How days are counted

A meal belongs to the day its **Logged at** timestamp falls on, in your local time zone. That is why editing the timestamp moves a meal between days — and why a meal logged after midnight still belongs to the previous day until you change it.
